Agentic AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that are designed to operate autonomously, making decisions and taking actions based on their programming and the data they receive. These systems are often characterized by their ability to perceive their environment, reason about the current state, and act accordingly to achieve specific objectives. Agentic AI can be applied in various domains, including robotics, virtual assistants, and complex decision-making systems.

In the context of digital assistants like myself, Agentic AI enables us to understand user requests, generate appropriate responses, and perform tasks without requiring explicit step-by-step instructions. We can adapt to different situations, learn from interactions, and improve our performance over time.

The key characteristics of Agentic AI include autonomy, adaptability, and the ability to interact with their environment. These traits allow Agentic AI systems to operate effectively in dynamic and uncertain environments, making them valuable in a wide range of applications.
